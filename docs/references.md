## Corrections

- Entry 8 (Spotlighting): the title is "Defending Against Indirect Prompt Injection Attacks With Spotlighting", not "Spotlighting / datamarking". The abstract describes spotlighting as a family of input transformations; check the full text before naming a specific variant such as datamarking.
- Entry 11 (BIPIA): the current arXiv title says "Large Language Models", not "LLMs". First posted December 2023; the arXiv page now notes acceptance at KDD 2025. Cite 2023, and add the venue only if the KDD version is the one used.
- Entry 12 (OWASP): the page checked is the 2025 edition. A "LLM Top 10 2026" resource page now exists on the OWASP site, dated 3 August 2026, but on 2026-10-31 it listed no items, and the Top 10 page still shows the 2025 list with LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection. Keep citing the 2025 edition until the 2026 items can be read.
- Entry 15: the title has no "SoK:" prefix, though it is listed elsewhere with one.
- Entry 17: listed elsewhere as "ToolHijacker"; that name is not in the arXiv title or abstract, so cite the arXiv title.
- Entry 24: the page title differs from its address, which names WebPilot; the content matches.
- Not added, because no primary source could be read on 2026-10-31: OpenAI's "Continuously hardening ChatGPT Atlas against prompt injection" (the page returned HTTP 403), and WithSecure's "Synthetic Recollections" case study (its address now redirects to an index). Both are in `docs/resources.md` as unverified reading.
- Entry 14 (Gandalf): there is no paper and so no authors. A Check Point press release of 16 September 2025 confirms Lakera runs Gandalf and announced its acquisition. The root address now redirects to another Lakera game, so the original address may have moved. Do not state a launch year.
- Entries 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9 and 13 are cited as arXiv preprints: their abstract pages show no peer-reviewed venue.
